Privacy Policy

AI Report Bridge is a personal, noncommercial application operated by its owner to transfer selected research reports from a local computer to the owner's Google Drive. This policy describes the application and this informational website.

Google authorization and access

The local application uses rclone and Google OAuth with the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. This permission concerns files created by the application or explicitly made accessible to it; it does not grant unrestricted access to every Drive file. Authorization takes place through Google's authorization flow, not through this website. The application does not collect the owner's Google password.

How report data is used

The application uploads selected report files and synchronization metadata to Google Drive. It reads file names, sizes, identifiers and folder listings to check uploads. It also downloads uploaded report contents and metadata for byte-length and SHA256 integrity checks. These operations support backup, synchronization verification and the owner's research review.

Storage and retention

OAuth tokens and application configuration are stored locally on the owner's computer. Report staging copies, verification downloads and operational logs are also stored locally. Logs and manifests can contain report names, identifiers, sizes, checksums and operation timestamps. Uploaded reports remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner removes them. The owner manages retention of local copies and logs; there is no automatic deletion period promised by this application.

Sharing and disclosure

The application sends reports to Google Drive as directed by its owner. It does not intentionally publish reports or create public sharing links. Existing Drive sharing settings and any sharing or connected services separately authorized by the owner still apply. The application does not sell Google user data or transfer it to advertisers. There is no advertising or advertising analytics integration in the application or these pages.

Website visitors

This website provides information only. It has no visitor accounts, registration, Google sign-in, report-upload forms, analytics scripts or third-party JavaScript libraries. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical request data such as IP addresses and maintain operational or security logs under its own privacy statement. The absence of application analytics does not mean that the hosting provider processes no visitor data.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can review or revoke AI Report Bridge access in Google Account third-party connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not itself delete existing Drive reports or local copies. Those can be removed separately by the owner. Local credentials should be kept private.
