Purpose and permitted use

AI Report Bridge is a personal, noncommercial tool for transferring selected research reports to the owner's own Google Drive. This website explains the tool; it does not offer public account registration, a hosted backup service, trading or financial advice.

Owner's responsibilities

The owner controls the local application, authorized Google account, selected files and Drive sharing settings. Only files the owner is entitled to process should be transferred. Credentials must be kept private. Google and GitHub services remain subject to their respective terms.

Availability and limitations

The tool depends on the local computer, network, rclone and Google services. Transfers may be delayed or fail. Verification checks help detect transfer errors but do not guarantee uninterrupted operation, permanent storage or recovery of every report. The owner should keep independent copies of important data. No trading results or financial returns are promised.

Stopping use

The owner can stop using the application and revoke its Google access. Revoking access does not automatically remove previously stored files. See the Privacy Policy for data handling and deletion information.
